Data Analyst
  • Day-to-day: Extracting, cleaning, and analyzing datasets to answer business questions.
  • Technical skills: SQL, Python, Tableau, Excel.
  • Entry-level expectations: Ability to write SQL queries, clean data, and present findings.
  • Salary & growth: $65,000 - $85,000
  • Strong candidate traits: Detail-oriented, curious, and business-focused.
Business Analyst
  • Day-to-day: Bridging the gap between IT and business, gathering requirements, and mapping processes.
  • Technical skills: Visio, Agile/Scrum, Jira, basic SQL.
  • Entry-level expectations: Ability to write user stories and communicate technical constraints clearly.
  • Salary & growth: $65,000 - $75,000
  • Strong candidate traits: Strong communicator, active listener, diplomatic.
Software Developer
  • Day-to-day: Writing, testing, and debugging code for internal applications or customer-facing products.
  • Technical skills: Python, Java, Git, HTML/CSS/JS.
  • Entry-level expectations: Understanding of object-oriented programming and basic version control.
  • Salary & growth: $70,000 - $90,000
  • Strong candidate traits: Persistent problem solver, highly receptive to code reviews.
Cybersecurity Analyst
  • Day-to-day: Monitoring networks for security breaches, investigating alerts, and updating protocols.
  • Technical skills: SIEM tools, Wireshark, TCP/IP, Python.
  • Entry-level expectations: Basic understanding of network architecture and common vulnerabilities.
  • Salary & growth: $70,000 - $85,000
  • Strong candidate traits: High ethical standards, calm under pressure, analytical.
Product Manager
  • Day-to-day: Defining product vision, prioritizing feature backlogs, and guiding development teams.
  • Technical skills: Roadmapping tools, Agile methodology, basic data analysis, UI/UX principles.
  • Entry-level expectations: Ability to balance user needs with business goals and technical feasibility.
  • Salary & growth: $80,000 - $100,000
  • Strong candidate traits: Empathetic to users, decisive, and strong leadership skills.
Technology Consultant
  • Day-to-day: Advising external clients on implementing software systems to meet organizational objectives.
  • Technical skills: ERP systems, Cloud platforms, presentation software, project management.
  • Entry-level expectations: Willingness to travel, rapid learning of new industries, and client-facing communication.
  • Salary & growth: $75,000 - $95,000
  • Strong candidate traits: Adaptable, maintains professional demeanor, strategic thinker.
